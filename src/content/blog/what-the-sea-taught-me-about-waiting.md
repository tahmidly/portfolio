The first time I stood in front of the sea for a long time, what struck me was not its size but its rhythm. Waves come in, pull back, and come in again. Nothing you do changes the timing.

## Things that can't be rushed

So much of life works the same way. Skills, friendships, trust, the right idea — they arrive when they are ready, not when we are impatient for them.

## Learning to wait well

Waiting well does not mean doing nothing. It means doing the small things — showing up, practising, paying attention — and then letting the tide do its part.
