Somewhere on the road into the hills, my phone quietly lost signal. For the first hour I kept checking it out of habit. By the second day I had forgotten it was in my pocket.

## The first quiet day

Without notifications, the days felt longer. Mornings started with mist sitting in the valley and ended with the sound of insects instead of a screen. Even meals felt slower.

## Coming back

The messages were all waiting when I came down — and none of them were urgent. That was the real lesson: most things can wait a couple of days. Our attention, it turns out, is the thing that can't be replaced.
