Some books you read once and move on. Others become a kind of home you keep coming back to, finding something new each time because you have changed since the last visit.

## Why re-reading is underrated

The second or third time through a good book, the story gets out of the way. You start noticing the small sentences, the ideas you skipped, the parts that suddenly feel written for exactly where you are now.

## How I keep a small shelf

I keep a short shelf of books within reach of my desk — only the ones I would happily read again. When the shelf gets full, something has to leave. It keeps the list honest.
