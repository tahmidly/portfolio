Some Sundays I try to do absolutely nothing useful. No errands, no catching up, no list. Just a slow morning and whatever the day turns into.

It is harder than it sounds. The first hour always feels a bit guilty, like I am forgetting something important. Then it passes, and the day opens up.

## What a slow day looks like

- A late breakfast and a second cup of coffee
- A walk with no destination
- Reading a few pages of a book I am not trying to finish
- An afternoon nap, without apology

## Why it matters

Rest is not the opposite of work — it is what makes the rest of the week possible. A slow Sunday is how I come back to Monday with something left in the tank.
